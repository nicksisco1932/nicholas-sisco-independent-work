## How to use it

- Choose a protein-containing food, a carbohydrate source suited to the meal, produce, fat and flavor. One food may occupy several roles.
- Use package information for the actual portion. Multiply per-serving values by portions used and add ingredient totals; do not mix dry and cooked weights.
- Swap according to preference, availability, allergy or dietary needs. A meal template is not proof of complete daily nutrient adequacy.

<aside class="callout"><h2>Worked example</h2><p>Illustrative labels: 200 g yogurt at 10 g protein/100 g gives 20 g; 40 g oats at 5 g/40 g gives 5 g; 20 g nuts at 4 g/20 g gives 4 g. Total: 29 g protein before fruit. Actual brands vary. A fortified soy option may differ substantially; check its label rather than assume equivalence.</p></aside>
