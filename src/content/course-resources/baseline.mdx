## How to use it

- Record three typical days. Approximate movement, sleep and meal descriptions are sufficient; no calorie or body-weight tracking is required.
- Use the same definitions across days and note estimates. Change an uncomfortable routine when needed; recording does not require preserving it.
- Choose one repeatable action, a smaller fallback and a review date. Keep notes on paper or in your own chosen tool.

<aside class="callout"><h2>Worked example</h2><p>Tuesday: walked 12 minutes at lunch; about 6.5 hours asleep; bought lunch because nothing was prepared. Experiment: pack one convenient lunch on Wednesday. Fallback: choose a familiar shop meal. Review on Sunday.</p></aside>
