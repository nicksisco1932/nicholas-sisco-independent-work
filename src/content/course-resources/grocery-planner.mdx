## How to use it

- List what you will actually use, what you already have and your storage/cooking constraints.
- Record local date, currency, package price and usable portions. Cost per usable portion = package price ÷ usable portions.
- Keep nutrition, price, shelf life, preference and preparation time in separate columns. Examples below use invented prices, not a promised weekly budget.

<aside class="callout"><h2>Worked example</h2><p>A $3 pack makes 4 usable portions: $0.75 each. If one portion is wasted, cost per eaten portion becomes $3 ÷ 3 = $1. A $4 ready-to-eat alternative making 4 eaten portions also costs $1 each. Nutrition and preparation requirements may still differ.</p></aside>
