## How to use it

- Choose one ability goal and a manageable schedule. Identify travel, work and other constraints first.
- Use the illustrative 12-week map or simpler repeated practice. Complex periodization is not required to participate effectively.
- At review points, compare similar tasks and revise the next block. A calendar is a plan, not a prediction of adaptation.

<aside class="callout"><h2>Worked example</h2><p>Weeks 1–4: establish repeatable practice (4 weeks). Weeks 5–8: progress when ready (4). Week 9: reduce demand if useful (1). Weeks 10–12: consolidate and review (3). Total 4 + 4 + 1 + 3 = 12. The reduced week is optional, not a physiological requirement.</p></aside>
