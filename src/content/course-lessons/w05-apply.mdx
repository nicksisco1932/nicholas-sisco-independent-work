Allow roughly 8–15 minutes with the activity. Suggested prerequisite: [Recovery is more than a feeling](w05-understand.html).

## Protect the opportunity

Identify one practical sleep constraint: a late task, noise, an inconsistent schedule or caregiving. Choose an action that is within your control. A stable wind-down cue may help organize the evening, but no routine guarantees sleep or overrides shift work and family responsibilities.

## Reduce demand deliberately

On a difficult day, use the starter guide’s shorter session, a lighter variation or easy comfortable activity. Avoid compensating for a missed session by doubling the next one. When illness or symptoms make activity inappropriate, rest and appropriate assessment take priority.

## Change one controllable feature

The recovery worksheet separates observation, possible explanation and action. Choose one change and a review point. If several changes are necessary, record them and accept that the result will not isolate one cause.
