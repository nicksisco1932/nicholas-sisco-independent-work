## Transport is regulated

Glucose can enter muscle through transport proteins. GLUT4 availability at the cell surface is regulated by insulin and by contraction-related processes. These routes overlap in their endpoint but are not identical pathways.

## A tissue is part of a system

Blood glucose reflects entry from food and liver output as well as removal by tissues. Greater uptake in an active muscle therefore does not by itself determine a person’s blood glucose, diagnosis or medication needs.

## Keep the measurement in view

A study can measure signaling, transporter abundance, glucose uptake or clinical control. Those measurements answer different questions. Human clamp research has found impaired uptake without proportionate defects in some measured signaling steps—another reason not to treat phosphorylation as the outcome.

<aside class="callout"><h2>Worked example</h2><p>A study measures more glucose entering a leg during a laboratory test. That supports an uptake observation; it does not establish diabetes remission or a rule that all carbohydrates must be eaten after training.</p></aside>
