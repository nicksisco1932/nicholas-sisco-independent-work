## Define the ability first

If the question is whether a familiar task feels easier, a repeatable submaximal check can answer it. A maximal lift answers a different question and adds technical and fatigue demands. This course does not require a one-repetition maximum or a set continued through form breakdown.

## Standardize what you can

Use the same variation, load, comfortable range and approximate rest. Choose a repetition cap that is already familiar and leave the intended reserve. Record effort and control. Stop earlier if symptoms or technique make continuing inappropriate.

## Interpret the result at its scale

Lower perceived effort at the same task is a useful observation. It is not a direct measurement of muscle size, longevity or metabolic health. A single day remains sensitive to sleep, practice, food and ordinary variation.
