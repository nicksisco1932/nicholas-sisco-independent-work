Allow roughly 10–15 minutes with the activity. Suggested prerequisite: [Build a minimum workable week](w10-apply.html).

## Count opportunities as well as actions

Three completed sessions mean different things if three or six realistic opportunities existed. Record interruptions when useful. Avoid interpreting participation as a direct measure of willpower, health or physiological progress.

## Ask what the friction reveals

Repeated late meals may suggest a shopping or preparation issue. Repeated missed gym visits may indicate a transport problem. These are examples to investigate, not automatic explanations. Ask what would make the next attempt easier and whether that change is feasible.

## Keep evidence and values visible

A plan can be scientifically reasonable yet personally unacceptable because of cost, enjoyment or competing priorities. Those considerations belong in the decision. Change the plan when needed rather than inventing a biological reason to justify a preference.
