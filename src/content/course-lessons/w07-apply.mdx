Allow roughly 8–15 minutes with the activity. Suggested prerequisite: [Fatigue is an observation, not a diagnosis](w07-understand.html).

## Choose what to reduce

A deload is a planned or responsive reduction in training demand. You might do fewer sets, reduce resistance or omit a demanding variation. Its purpose is practical load management; it is not proof of a hormonal reset. Beginners do not automatically require a deload on a fixed calendar.

## Keep the choice interpretable

For the starter program, reducing two sets to one gives a simple, visible change. Keep the movement comfortable, avoid progression and record the adjustment. If a movement provokes pain, substituting or stopping is more appropriate than completing it because the session is labeled easy.

## Review before rebuilding

Set a review point after the reduced period. Consider ordinary function, symptoms and readiness for usual activity. Return gradually if appropriate; do not make up omitted sets. Lack of improvement or worsening symptoms changes the decision toward further assessment.
