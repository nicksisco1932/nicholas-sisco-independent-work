Allow roughly 10–15 minutes with the activity. Suggested prerequisite: [Compare foods on equal terms](w02-apply.html).

## Name the actual constraint

A cheaper package may require refrigeration, a larger upfront payment or cooking time you do not have. A more convenient food can be a useful choice. The relevant cost includes waste and effort, not only the shelf price.

## Check the arithmetic

Multiply nutrient amount per portion by the number of portions actually used. Keep dry and cooked weights separate. If a basket is meant for a week, divide weekly totals by seven before comparing them with any daily example. Do not infer nutritional completeness from a list of food names.

## Leave room for flexibility

You do not need to earn enjoyable food by first meeting a rigid rule. Choose a workable pattern with variety and adequate intake, then revise it using practical observations. No food comparison in this course produces a diagnosis or a moral score.

<aside class="callout"><h2>Worked example</h2><p>An illustrative pack costs $3 and supplies four usable portions: $0.75 per portion. If each portion contains 10 g protein, the package supplies 40 g. A second pack with half its contents wasted may cost more per portion actually eaten even if its shelf price is lower.</p></aside>

## Evidence & reading

1. <span id="ref-R19"></span>[FDA, How to Understand and Use the Nutrition Facts Label](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/how-understand-and-use-nutrition-facts-label)
Government educational guidance. Population:  US food-label users. Endpoint: Serving-size and nutrient comparisons.Limit: Label-based comparisons need consistent units; ingredient familiarity is not a validated health score.
2. <span id="ref-R24"></span>[ISSN position stand: protein and exercise (2017)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28642676/)
Position stand / synthesis. Population:  Healthy exercising people. Endpoint: Protein intake and adaptation.Limit: Athletic guidance differs from minimum adequacy and clinical needs.
3. <span id="ref-R01"></span>[Morton et al. (2018), protein supplementation and resistance training](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2017-097608)
Human intervention synthesis. Population:  Healthy adults undertaking resistance training. Endpoint: Fat-free mass and strength.Limit: Group-level dose-response estimate; not a universal minimum, not a lean-mass denominator.
