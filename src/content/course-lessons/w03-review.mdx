## Look for a workable combination

A training plan can fail because the day has no time to eat or because the session ends too late for sleep. The solution may be a shorter session, a portable meal or a different day. More precision in one component cannot fix an impossible schedule.

## Review the plan at the level you control

Ask whether you had the equipment, food and time you expected. Then review how practice felt. Avoid inventing a molecular explanation for every good or bad day. Keep the parts that fit before adding complexity.
