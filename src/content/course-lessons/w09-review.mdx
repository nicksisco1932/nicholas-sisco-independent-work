## Locate the leap

Consider the headline: this workout sends all carbohydrate to muscle and reverses diabetes. It jumps from a possible tissue response to universal nutrient partitioning and a clinical outcome. Neither follows automatically from the mechanism.

## State what would be needed

To support a clinical claim, research needs defined populations, a comparison, relevant measurements over time and appropriate clinical criteria. Medication use and other changes matter. A pathway diagram cannot supply those data.

## Keep a useful conclusion

You can retain the supported idea that physical activity affects muscle glucose handling while removing the universal promise. Precise wording does not make the biology less interesting; it makes the claim testable and useful.

<aside class="callout"><h2>Worked example</h2><p>Revised headline: Muscle activity can change glucose uptake; the effect on longer-term clinical outcomes depends on the population, intervention and measured endpoint.</p></aside>

## Try it

- Underline each unsupported step in the original headline.
- Write a two-sentence correction naming the mechanism and the missing clinical evidence.
