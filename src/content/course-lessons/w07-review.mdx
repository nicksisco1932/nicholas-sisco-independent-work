Allow roughly 10–15 minutes with the activity. Suggested prerequisite: [Design a reduced-demand week](w07-apply.html).

## Start with the facts

Separate a missed target from a symptom. Missing two repetitions alone is not equivalent to dizziness, chest pain or inability to perform usual daily tasks. Record onset, duration and relevant changes without assigning a disease label.

## Use three possible actions

Repeat or reduce ordinary training when the pattern is mild and temporary. Seek professional assessment for persistent or unexplained problems. Seek urgent help for serious symptoms. These are decision categories, not a complete screening instrument.

## Keep the referral informative

If assessment is needed, concise observations help: what changed, for how long, what makes it worse and what adjustments you tried. Avoid arriving with a course-generated hormone diagnosis or treating a supplement trial as a required step first.

<aside class="callout"><h2>Worked example</h2><p>Morgan remains unusually fatigued for several weeks despite a reduced schedule. The next assignment is to arrange assessment and describe the pattern, not to conduct a heavier workout as a test.</p></aside>
