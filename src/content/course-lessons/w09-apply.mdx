Allow roughly 8–15 minutes with the activity. Suggested prerequisite: [Muscle takes up fuel through regulated routes](w09-understand.html).

## Use the schedule you already have

Return to the Week 6 meal plan. Place usual meals and manageable activity where they fit. The physiological response to exercise does not create a requirement to reserve all carbohydrate for a short post-workout interval.

## Choose a practical movement option

If comfortable and appropriate, use a brief easy walk or other accessible activity as part of the day. Its value in this assignment is making a workable routine. Do not infer a personal glucose effect without appropriate measurement, and do not use activity to compensate for eating.

## Keep clinical management separate

People using medicines that affect glucose may need individualized exercise and food guidance. This general course does not set glucose thresholds, adjust medication or prescribe carbohydrate corrections. Use the fictional scenario if those decisions are relevant.
