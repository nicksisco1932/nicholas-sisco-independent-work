Allow roughly 8–15 minutes with the activity. Suggested prerequisite: [A response is not yet an adaptation](w03-understand.html).

## Start from the pattern

The starter guide offers two full-body sessions with home and gym choices. Select options you can set up securely and perform through a comfortable range. Learn the movement before increasing external load. Equipment differences do not require a different course.

## Use a modest entry

The course’s starter prescription is an educational starting point, not an individualized optimum. Begin with the guide’s low end, leave repetitions in reserve, and allow recovery between sessions. A completed manageable session provides information for the next attempt.

## Include the rest of the week

Resistance exercise is one part of physical activity. Walking or other comfortable aerobic activity can fit around it. Meals and sleep should support the schedule rather than become additional tests you must pass. Do not compensate for a missed workout by stacking hard sessions.
