Allow roughly 10–15 minutes with the activity. Suggested prerequisite: [Write a plan you can actually review](w12-apply.html).

## Explain a complete decision

Choose one course decision and explain it from observation to action: what you saw, the mechanism that may matter, the human evidence, the uncertainty and the practical adjustment. You do not need to name every molecule to reason well.

## Audit your own wording

Replace an unsupported guarantee with a bounded statement. Distinguish participation from outcome and plausible mechanism from demonstrated effect. A good explanation can say what remains unknown without becoming indecisive.

## Continue at the appropriate depth

Repeat a useful week, follow the next cycle or use the Molecular Physiology atlas for deeper questions. Completion marks are self-reported participation. They are not an accredited qualification, verified competency assessment or evidence of a physiological transformation.

## Try it

- Write your own five-sentence decision explanation using observation, mechanism, evidence, uncertainty and action.
- Choose your next learning or practice step and a date to review it.
