Allow roughly 10–15 minutes with the activity. Suggested prerequisite: [Build two workable food schedules](w06-apply.html).

## Use the evidence you have

Review the first six weeks for decisions that became easier: choosing meals, recording a session, holding a load or taking a reduced day. Completion does not imply a particular body-composition change. The route title is a learning theme, not a promised outcome.

## Choose one next experiment

Keep the training pattern stable while changing one manageable feature, such as preparing a backup meal or using a more consistent rest interval. Write the expected observation and a date to review it. A result that does not match expectations still teaches you something.

## Decide whether to continue

You can repeat these weeks, keep using the starter program, or continue into evidence literacy and self-management. No supplement purchase, maximal test or body measurement is required to move forward.
