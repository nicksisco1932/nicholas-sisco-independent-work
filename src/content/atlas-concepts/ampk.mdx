## Level 1 · Foundations

Building and maintaining cells costs energy. AMPK helps adjust cellular activity when energy demand challenges supply.

## Level 2 · Mechanism

Changes in adenine nucleotides and upstream phosphorylation regulate AMPK. AMPK can restrain mTORC1 signaling through phosphorylation of RAPTOR. [Gwinn DM 2008](#ref-gwinn2008)

<details class="advanced"><summary>Level 3 · Advanced</summary><p>AMP, ADP, and ATP interact with regulatory sites; nucleotide state and kinase/phosphatase activity jointly matter. Bulk ATP alone is an incomplete description of energetic state. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-gwinn2008">Gwinn DM 2008</a></span></p></details>

## Inputs

- Adenine nucleotide balance
- Upstream kinases
- Cellular stress

## Outputs

- Energy-conserving responses
- RAPTOR regulation

## Necessary versus sufficient

- **Contributory**: Energy-sensitive regulation; AMPK activity is not sufficient to predict training interference.

## Human evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Mechanistic inference</span></div><p class="small">Model: Human · Directness: Inferred</p><p>A cellular inhibitory connection cannot by itself establish the outcome of concurrent training in people. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-gwinn2008">Gwinn DM 2008</a></span></p></div>

## Model-system evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Cell culture</span></div><p class="small">Model: Cellular · Directness: Direct</p><p>RAPTOR phosphorylation experiments support an AMPK-mediated restraint on mTORC1. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-gwinn2008">Gwinn DM 2008</a></span></p></div>

## What remains unresolved

Compartment, exercise dose, and recovery change how molecular responses relate to adaptation.

<aside class="callout"><h2>A common oversimplification</h2><p class="misconception">"AMPK cancels muscle growth."</p><p><strong>Correction:</strong> Energy regulation is dynamic; one inhibitory interaction does not determine a whole training outcome.</p></aside>

## Practical relevance

Interpret energetic stress within the complete training and recovery context.
