## Level 1 · Foundations

Cells can change how many protein-making machines they have. This differs from changing how quickly existing machines work.

## Level 2 · Mechanism

Ribosomal RNA production, processing, and assembly with ribosomal proteins build translational capacity. [Stec MJ 2016](#ref-stec2016)

<details class="advanced"><summary>Level 3 · Advanced</summary><p>Pre-rRNA, total RNA, assembled ribosome content, and active translation measure different things. Total RNA is a proxy for capacity, not a direct count of productive ribosomes. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-stec2016">Stec MJ 2016</a></span></p></details>

## Inputs

- Transcriptional regulation
- Nucleotides
- Ribosomal proteins
- Energy

## Outputs

- Ribosomal subunits
- Potential translational capacity

## Necessary versus sufficient

- **Correlated**: Human training studies associate ribosomal measures with growth.
- **Required**: Inhibition experiments support a requirement in tested myotube growth conditions; this is not a universal human threshold.

## Human evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Human intervention</span><span>Human biopsy</span></div><p class="small">Model: Human · Directness: Indirect</p><p>Human training responses associate ribosomal measures with hypertrophy heterogeneity. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-stec2016">Stec MJ 2016</a></span></p></div>

## Model-system evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Cell culture</span></div><p class="small">Model: Cellular · Directness: Direct</p><p>In vitro inhibition experiments tested dependence of myotube growth on ribosome biogenesis. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-stec2016">Stec MJ 2016</a></span></p></div>

## What remains unresolved

The extent to which ribosomal capacity limits different human training responses is unresolved.

<aside class="callout"><h2>A common oversimplification</h2><p class="misconception">"More RNA always means more muscle."</p><p><strong>Correction:</strong> Capacity, active use, and net protein balance are different quantities.</p></aside>

## Practical relevance

Separate translational capacity from acute signaling when reading adaptation studies.
