## Level 1 · Foundations

Before a protein can be built, the cell must begin reading its RNA instructions. 4E-BP1 helps regulate access to that starting machinery.

## Level 2 · Mechanism

Binding of 4E-BP1 to eIF4E restrains eIF4F assembly. mTORC1-associated phosphorylation can reduce this restraint. [Thoreen CC 2012](#ref-thoreen2012)

<details class="advanced"><summary>Level 3 · Advanced</summary><p>Multiple phosphorylation sites and competing protein interactions influence eIF4E availability. Measuring one site is not equivalent to measuring initiation across all messenger RNAs. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-thoreen2012">Thoreen CC 2012</a></span></p></details>

## Inputs

- mTORC1 signaling
- Phosphatase activity

## Outputs

- eIF4E availability
- Cap-dependent initiation

## Necessary versus sufficient

- **Permissive**: Relief of inhibition supports initiation but does not guarantee complete protein production.

## Human evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Human biopsy</span></div><p class="small">Model: Human · Directness: Indirect</p><p>Translation signaling can be sampled in muscle, but a single regulator cannot report total synthesis flux. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-drummond2009">Drummond MJ 2009</a></span></p></div>

## Model-system evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Cell culture</span></div><p class="small">Model: Cellular · Directness: Direct</p><p>Perturbation and translation profiling support an important 4E-BP-dependent branch of mTORC1 control. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-thoreen2012">Thoreen CC 2012</a></span></p></div>

## What remains unresolved

Responses vary across transcripts and conditions; cell-wide conclusions require more than a phosphoblot.

<aside class="callout"><h2>A common oversimplification</h2><p class="misconception">"Phosphorylated 4E-BP1 means every protein is being made faster."</p><p><strong>Correction:</strong> Initiation is transcript-sensitive and remains subject to other constraints.</p></aside>

## Practical relevance

Look for synthesis measurements alongside signaling measurements.
