## Level 1 · Foundations

Muscle continually replaces and remodels proteins. Making new protein can repair existing structures as well as support growth.

## Level 2 · Mechanism

Ribosomes link amino acids into sequences specified by messenger RNA. Synthesis must be considered alongside protein breakdown to interpret net protein balance.

<details class="advanced"><summary>Level 3 · Advanced</summary><p>Stable-isotope incorporation estimates fractional synthesis over a defined interval and protein pool. Precursor enrichment assumptions, biopsy timing, and pool selection affect interpretation; the estimate is not a direct measure of net accretion. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-churchward2012">Churchward-Venne TA 2012</a>; <a href="#ref-damas2016">Damas F 2016</a></span></p></details>

## Inputs

- Amino acids
- Energy
- Translational capacity and regulation

## Outputs

- New proteins

## Necessary versus sufficient

- **Required**: New protein production is required for protein accretion.
- **Correlated**: A synthesis measurement may relate to growth, but the relationship depends on when and how it is measured.

## Human evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Human intervention</span><span>Tracer study</span></div><p class="small">Model: Human · Directness: Direct</p><p>Integrated synthesis became related to hypertrophy after early damage attenuated in one training study. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-damas2016">Damas F 2016</a></span></p></div>

## Model-system evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Mechanistic inference</span></div><p class="small">Model: Cellular · Directness: Inferred</p><p>Translation regulation provides a mechanism, but cellular regulation does not determine an individual's growth response. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-thoreen2012">Thoreen CC 2012</a></span></p></div>

## What remains unresolved

How short measurement windows represent weeks of synthesis and breakdown remains a central inference problem.

<aside class="callout"><h2>A common oversimplification</h2><p class="misconception">"A higher acute synthesis measurement proves more hypertrophy."</p><p><strong>Correction:</strong> Repair, turnover, and timing can separate acute synthesis from later growth.</p></aside>

## Practical relevance

Prefer outcome-matched evidence when evaluating a long-term claim.
