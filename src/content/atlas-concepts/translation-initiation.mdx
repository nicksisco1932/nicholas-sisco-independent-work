## Level 1 · Foundations

A ribosome must locate a message and establish where to start reading it. Initiation organizes that beginning.

## Level 2 · Mechanism

For cap-dependent initiation, eIF4F helps recruit machinery to messenger RNA. The 4E-BP–eIF4E interaction regulates access to this process. [Thoreen CC 2012](#ref-thoreen2012)

<details class="advanced"><summary>Level 3 · Advanced</summary><p>Initiation rate is only one constraint on protein production. Charged transfer RNAs, elongation, energy supply, and quality control are also needed to finish a polypeptide.</p></details>

## Inputs

- Messenger RNA
- Initiation factors
- Ribosomal subunits
- Energy and amino-acid availability

## Outputs

- Ribosomes committed to elongation

## Necessary versus sufficient

- **Required**: Starting translation is required to produce a new polypeptide; increased initiation is not sufficient for net tissue accretion.

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Tracer study</span></div><p class="small">Model: Human · Directness: Indirect</p><p>Tracer incorporation estimates synthesis downstream of initiation without isolating initiation rate. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-churchward2012">Churchward-Venne TA 2012</a></span></p></div>

## What remains unresolved

Rate-limiting steps can differ between messages and physiological states.

<aside class="callout"><h2>A common oversimplification</h2><p class="misconception">"Starting translation guarantees muscle growth."</p><p><strong>Correction:</strong> Completion, protein fate, and degradation determine the resulting balance.</p></aside>

## Practical relevance

Distinguish regulatory machinery from measured production.
