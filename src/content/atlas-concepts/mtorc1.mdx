## Level 1 · Foundations

Cells coordinate construction with available resources. mTORC1 helps regulate that coordination; it does not manufacture muscle by itself.

## Level 2 · Mechanism

Rag-dependent recruitment and Rheb-dependent activation converge on mTORC1. Its substrates include S6K1 and 4E-BP proteins involved in translation regulation. [Sancak Y 2008](#ref-sancak2008); [Yang H 2017](#ref-yang2017); [Thoreen CC 2012](#ref-thoreen2012)

<details class="advanced"><summary>Level 3 · Advanced</summary><p>Localization changes encounter probabilities; allostery changes catalysis; substrate recognition changes which reactions occur. These are separable levels of control, not interchangeable meanings of activation. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-docking2019">Rogala KB 2019</a>; <a href="#ref-yang2017">Yang H 2017</a></span></p></details>

## Inputs

- Amino-acid sensing
- Rheb
- Energetic state
- Loading-associated signals

## Outputs

- Translation regulation
- Cellular growth and turnover programs

## Necessary versus sufficient

- **Required**: Rapamycin-sensitive signaling is required for the early synthesis increase in a specific human exercise experiment, not proven sufficient for hypertrophy.

## Human evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Human intervention</span><span>Human biopsy</span><span>Tracer study</span></div><p class="small">Model: Human · Directness: Direct</p><p>Rapamycin blocked the early exercise-induced synthesis increase in the tested protocol. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-drummond2009">Drummond MJ 2009</a></span></p></div>

## Model-system evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Biochemical reconstitution</span></div><p class="small">Model: Biochemical · Directness: Direct</p><p>Structural work resolves mechanisms for recruitment and catalytic regulation. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-yang2017">Yang H 2017</a>; <a href="#ref-docking2019">Rogala KB 2019</a></span></p></div>

## What remains unresolved

Acute pharmacological dependence does not quantify long-term mediation of training adaptation.

<aside class="callout"><h2>A common oversimplification</h2><p class="misconception">"More mTORC1 signaling means more muscle."</p><p><strong>Correction:</strong> Signal duration, substrates, turnover, and repeated exposures separate a kinase response from tissue growth.</p></aside>

## Practical relevance

Treat pathway biomarkers as mechanistic observations with bounded meaning.

## Neighboring concepts

- **Small GTPases** — [Rag GTPases](rag-gtpases.html): Heterodimeric nucleotide-binding proteins that help recruit mTORC1 to lysosomal membranes.

- **Small GTPase** — [Rheb](rheb.html): A small GTPase that can directly stimulate the catalytic activity of mTORC1.

- **Kinase** — [S6K1](s6k1.html): A kinase downstream of mTORC1 that participates in regulation of protein synthesis and cellular signaling.

- **Translation regulator** — [4E-BP1](4e-bp1.html): An eIF4E-binding protein that regulates assembly of the cap-dependent translation initiation machinery.

- **Tissue adaptation** — [Hypertrophy](hypertrophy.html): A sustained increase in tissue or cell size; here, growth of skeletal muscle fibers and muscle tissue.
