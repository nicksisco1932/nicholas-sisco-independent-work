## Level 1 · Foundations

Putting an enzyme in the right place does not guarantee that it is active. Rheb provides an activation input to mTORC1.

## Level 2 · Mechanism

GTP-bound Rheb binds mTORC1 and promotes catalytic activation. TSC restrains Rheb through GTPase-activating activity. [Yang H 2017](#ref-yang2017); [Inoki K 2002](#ref-inoki2002)

<details class="advanced"><summary>Level 3 · Advanced</summary><p>Structural work supports allosteric rearrangement of mTOR following Rheb binding. The reaction depends on nucleotide state and productive encounters, not simply total Rheb abundance. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-yang2017">Yang H 2017</a></span></p></details>

## Inputs

- TSC complex
- GTP/GDP state
- Membrane association

## Outputs

- mTORC1 kinase activation

## Necessary versus sufficient

- **Permissive**: For nutrient-responsive pathway operation.
- **Sufficient**: To stimulate isolated mTORC1 in defined biochemical assays; not sufficient for organismal growth.

## Human evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Mechanistic inference</span></div><p class="small">Model: Human · Directness: Inferred</p><p>Biochemical activation does not establish the size of Rheb's contribution to training outcomes. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-yang2017">Yang H 2017</a></span></p></div>

## Model-system evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Biochemical reconstitution</span></div><p class="small">Model: Biochemical · Directness: Direct</p><p>Structural and kinase assays connect Rheb binding to mTORC1 activation. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-yang2017">Yang H 2017</a></span></p></div>

## What remains unresolved

Spatial dynamics during contraction in intact human muscle are less resolved than the purified mechanism.

<aside class="callout"><h2>A common oversimplification</h2><p class="misconception">"Leucine directly activates Rheb."</p><p><strong>Correction:</strong> The canonical nutrient-recruitment arm and TSC–Rheb activation arm are distinct interacting inputs.</p></aside>
