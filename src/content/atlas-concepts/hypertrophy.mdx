## Level 1 · Foundations

Muscle growth accumulates over time. A temporary pump or swelling after exercise is not the same observation as lasting tissue growth.

## Level 2 · Mechanism

Sustained protein accretion reflects synthesis exceeding breakdown over time, alongside other remodeling. A single bout samples only a small portion of that history. [Damas F 2016](#ref-damas2016)

<details class="advanced"><summary>Level 3 · Advanced</summary><p>Fiber cross-sectional area, whole-muscle volume, and lean mass differ in scale and specificity. Hydration and sampling affect measurements; strength also depends on neural and task-specific factors. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-mitchell2014">Mitchell CJ 2014</a>; <a href="#ref-damas2016">Damas F 2016</a></span></p></details>

## Inputs

- Repeated loading
- Protein balance
- Recovery and tissue remodeling

## Outputs

- Larger muscle fibers and tissue
- Potential contribution to force capacity

## Necessary versus sufficient

- **Contributory**: Muscle size contributes to force capacity but is not sufficient to specify task performance.

## Human evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Human intervention</span></div><p class="small">Model: Human · Directness: Direct</p><p>Longitudinal training studies measure growth; one acute synthesis response did not predict it in the cited cohort. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-mitchell2014">Mitchell CJ 2014</a></span></p></div>

## Model-system evidence

<div class="evidence"><div class="badges"><span>Cell culture</span></div><p class="small">Model: Cellular · Directness: Indirect</p><p>Myotube growth permits mechanistic perturbation but differs from mature human tissue adaptation. <span class="citations"><a href="#ref-stec2016">Stec MJ 2016</a></span></p></div>

## What remains unresolved

Individual adaptation integrates exposures and biology that are poorly captured by any single biomarker.

<aside class="callout"><h2>A common oversimplification</h2><p class="misconception">"Phosphorylation is muscle growth."</p><p><strong>Correction:</strong> A regulatory event and a lasting tissue phenotype require different measurements.</p></aside>

## Practical relevance

Judge long-term claims with longitudinal tissue and functional outcomes.
